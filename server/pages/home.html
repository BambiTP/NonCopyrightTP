How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD: move
  • Enter: chat with everyone, T: chat with your team, G: group chat
  • Space: use a held powerup (when the group allows it)
  • Esc: close chat, Tab: hold for the scoreboard
  • Touch the enemy flag to grab it, then touch your own flag to score.
  • Touch an enemy carrying your flag to pop them and send the flag home.
  • Boosts launch you; bombs blow everyone nearby away; spikes pop you.
  • Powerups: juke juice (more grip), rolling bomb (explodes on contact), tag pro (pop anyone you touch).
